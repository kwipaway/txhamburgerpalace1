About Us

Texas Hamburger Palace is a family owned business that has been serving the Houston area for over 20 years. We pride ourselves on providing fresh, quality food at a great value. Our menu features a variety of burgers, sandwiches, and sides that are sure to satisfy any appetite. We also offer catering services for your next event. Thank you for choosing Texas Hamburger Palace!
